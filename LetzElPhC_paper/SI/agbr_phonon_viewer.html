LetzElPhC: an electron–phonon code for efficient exciton–phonon workflows

Interactive viewer for the phonon dispersion and electronic band structure of AgBr

Supplemental Material to “LetzElPhC: an electron–phonon code for efficient exciton–phonon workflows”: an interactive version of Fig. 8 of the paper, rocksalt AgBr from the phonons to the phonon-assisted luminescence. All panels use the same path K–Γ–L–W–X and the same symmetry labels.

  • Phonon dispersion (Quantum ESPRESSO, LDA, with LO–TO splitting), in cm⁻¹ or meV, with the point group of the wave vector above the plot. Click a phonon to see its irreducible representation.
  • First Brillouin zone with the k-path; the red dot marks the wave vector of the selected phonon. Drag to rotate.
  • Mode animation of the selected phonon, with its character table. Drag the crystal to rotate it.
  • Electronic band structure (LDA) with the irreducible representation and orbital character of every Bloch state. Select a valence and a conduction state to get the symmetry of the electron–hole pair.
  • Quasiparticle band structure (G0W0) of the bands around the gap, with the same state and pair analysis.
  • Exciton dispersion (GW + Bethe–Salpeter equation), with the onset of the electron–hole continuum; the lowest exciton is the indirect one at Q = L.
  • Phonon-assisted luminescence, computed and measured, with the four phonon replicas of the indirect exciton; click a replica to animate its phonon.

▶Animate any phonon mode: click any point of the dispersion. The mode under the pointer then vibrates in the Mode animation panel below, and its wave vector is marked in the Brillouin zone.

Theory

Click a phonon replica (green) to animate its phonon at L in the Mode animation panel.

Symmetry labels. Labels of the high-symmetry points and lines as introduced by Bouckaert, Smoluchowski and Wigner[6]. Above the plot: point group of the wave vector (little group[2], Schoenflies notation) for the rocksalt space group Fm-3m, bold at the high-symmetry points (K C2v, Γ Oh, L D3d, W D2d, X D4h) and in italics on the lines between them (Σ C2v, Λ C3v, Q C2, Z C2v). The irreducible representation of a clicked mode is obtained from its eigenvector: the characters of the representation spanned by each set of degenerate modes under the operations of the little group, including the Bloch phase of the Br atom[1], reduced with the standard character tables[3, 4] (Mulliken labels[5]). For C2v, B1 and B2 refer to the mirrors named in the table header.

Path. K–Γ–L–W–X of the fcc Brillouin zone: K = (¾,¾,0), L = (½,½,½), W = (1,½,0), X = (1,0,0) up to cubic symmetry, here K = (−¾,¾,0), L = (−½,½,½), W = (−1,½,0), X = (−1,0,0) in units of 2π/a₀ (the points of the matdyn input). Γ appears twice: each copy is computed just off Γ along its own segment, so the LO–TO splitting appears with the limit for the direction of approach (from K, and towards L).

Animation. Atoms move along the real part of u·ei(q·R − ωt), with the Bloch phase from cell to cell; the pattern repeats with the wavelength 2π/|q|. Arrows show the instantaneous velocity of each atom. The block is a cube of edge a₀ (conventional rocksalt cell), repeated 1–3 times.
